# One tip per line; bluespin-motd shows one at random. Blank lines and
# # comments are ignored.
Alt+Tab switches windows, Super+Tab switches applications.
Ctrl+. opens a console in the current folder from Files.
Ctrl+Shift+Esc opens Mission Center.
Super+Space opens Search Light.
Super+. opens the Smile emoji picker.
`ujust enroll-secureboot-key` lets the signed kernel and virtual camera load with Secure Boot on.
`bootc rollback` undoes the last update.
`ujust install-flatpaks` adds the opt-in extra or dx app sets, per user.
`ujust distrobox-assemble` creates ready-made Arch, Debian or Fedora containers.
`ujust setup-luks-tpm-unlock` unlocks full-disk encryption with the TPM.
